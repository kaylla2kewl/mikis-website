Dear Miki Bear, 🩵

Happy one month since we confessed our feelings to each other, my Miki Bear. ♡

I've been thinking about that moment — how two people can be carrying feelings quietly, wondering what the other person might say, and then finally let the truth out. I'm really glad we did.

I don't know every chapter of our story yet, and honestly, that's part of what makes this beginning so lovely. We get to learn each other a little more, make memories, be silly, tease each other, have our little moments, and see where this sweet thing between us takes us.

And because I must remain at least a little annoying, I need you to know that your smile is a problem. A very serious one. You can be doing absolutely nothing, and somehow my brain goes, “Oh, look. It's Miki Bear again. Let's think about her for the next 47 business hours.” 🤭

I love the little things that make you you — your laugh, your expressions, your playful side, and the way even a tiny interaction can stay with me long after it's over. You don't have to be perfect or have everything figured out to be special to me. You can be sleepy, silly, grumpy, quiet, extra, or a tiny menace. I'll still be happy to know the real you. (Yes, even the side-eye. Especially the side-eye. 😌)

I don't want this little gift to make you feel pressured or like you have to promise me anything. I just wanted you to know that this first month has made me smile, and I'm grateful we trusted each other enough to be honest about our feelings.

So here's to our little beginning: to more laughter, more teasing, more honest conversations, and all the lovely little moments we haven't met yet. One step at a time, at a pace that feels right for both of us. 🩵

♡ From Shé, For Miki Bear.
