It's a little crazy to think about how much can change when you finally find the courage to say what has been sitting in your heart. One month ago, I decided to be honest with you about my feelings, and that moment became the beginning of something that means so much to me.

This is my little way of keeping one of those moments somewhere we can look back on. 🩵

With love,
